### Changed

- The pull-request changelog check now accepts a per-PR fragment, `changes/unreleased/<PR number>.md`, and no longer asks for a new line under `## [Unreleased]` in `CHANGELOG.md`. A PR that edits an Unreleased section directly is refused, with a message to move the entry into a fragment ([#10869](https://github.com/code-yeongyu/oh-my-openagent/pull/10869), [#10841](https://github.com/code-yeongyu/oh-my-openagent/issues/10841)).
