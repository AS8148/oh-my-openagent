**Completed Momus review verdicts are now persisted as numbered Markdown artifacts next to the plan file.** Each `.omo/plans/{plan}.reviews/NNN-okay.md` or `NNN-reject.md` artifact captures the verdict, timestamp, model, plan sha256, review duration, reviewer session id, and the verdict block (the reasoning that precedes the verdict is not stored), so a finished review survives the end of the session. A `.gitignore` written next to the plans keeps the `.reviews` directories out of the user's own staging area. Momus itself stays read-only; persistence happens entirely on the orchestrator side after the review result returns. ([#11237](https://github.com/code-yeongyu/oh-my-openagent/pull/11237), fixes [#3002](https://github.com/code-yeongyu/oh-my-openagent/issues/3002))
