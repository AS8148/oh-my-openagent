### Fixed

- Research fidelity verification binds the unit written right after a value-only fact, with or without a space and with a Korean particle, a currency sign before it, its unit placeholder, or its table column header by visual column, so hand-edited units block and ordinary prose such as "m in total length" no longer raises a false unit mismatch.
- First-person verification cues ("we verified", "we double-checked", and the Korean equivalents) warn only when the clause names a result and no document; one cue list covers English and Korean.
