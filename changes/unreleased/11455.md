**Kimi K3.1 Pro is the default Kimi model.** Every built-in chain and the Daily · Normal model profile that ran Kimi K3 now runs Kimi K3.1 Pro (`kimi-k3.1-pro`, `k3.1-pro` on Kimi Code) on Kimi Code, Moonshot, Moonshot CN and OpenCode Go. OpenCode Zen, Bailian, Firmware, Ollama Cloud and AIHubMix do not serve K3.1 Pro yet, so they keep Kimi K3 on a rung right after it. Kimi K3.1 Pro and K3.1 Pro Highspeed get capability entries (1M context), telemetry reports them by name, and the fallback notice calls the model "Kimi K3.1 Pro".
