**Maintainers can inspect or recreate a stuck get.omo.dev channel pointer from the installer mirror workflow.** A manual run with `op=head` prints the signed HEAD and GET of `channels/latest` or `channels/beta` (status, every response header, body; credentials redacted), and `op=recreate` deletes the pointer and writes the given, already mirrored version as a new object, then reads it back. ([#11349](https://github.com/code-yeongyu/oh-my-openagent/pull/11349), part of [#11313](https://github.com/code-yeongyu/oh-my-openagent/issues/11313))
