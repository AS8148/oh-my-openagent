**A permission denial or a refusal by the user is final for what the refused call was meant to do.** Every model route of Sisyphus, Sisyphus-Junior, Hephaestus and Atlas now ends its prompt (before any `prompt_append`) with one rule: never reach a refused result with another tool, command, path or host; ask the user. Every ultrawork variant (default, Gemini, GLM, GPT, Codex, planner) and the todo and boulder continuations carry the same rule. A test enumerates every prompt route, so a new route without the rule fails. The continuations say "Proceed without asking whether to continue" instead of "Proceed without asking for permission". Computer-use telemetry still counts a refusal as a permission denial when senpi adds guidance after its first sentence.
