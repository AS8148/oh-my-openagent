**A permission denial or a refusal by the user is final for what the refused call was meant to do.** Agent prompts that say to try another approach when blocked (ultrawork, Hephaestus, Sisyphus-Junior, the todo and boulder continuations) now tell the model never to reach a refused result with another tool, command, path or host, and to ask the user instead. The continuations say "Proceed without asking whether to continue" instead of "Proceed without asking for permission". Computer-use telemetry still counts a refusal as a permission denial when senpi adds guidance after its first sentence.
