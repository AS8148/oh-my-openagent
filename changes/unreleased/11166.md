**A ulw-research run cannot produce a reader-facing artifact against an undecided brief.** The deliverable interview now also records a `## Fidelity` block, and the new `scripts/fidelity-policy.mjs` registry turns it into the rules the CLI enforces: `fact fill`, `fact verify`, `fact pairs`, `fact meaning-check`, `fact polish-apply` and `fact polish-diff` refuse an unlocked brief with one `fidelity_interview_incomplete` defect and write nothing, each reading `--brief` (default `$SESSION_DIR/brief.md`), while collection keeps running; `fact fill` then refuses a derived row under `derived numbers: none`, evaluates the source floor live against each source's `meta.json`, binds a translation to its quote only under `languages ... with translation beside`, and omits a twice-failed sentence under `fallback: omit`.
